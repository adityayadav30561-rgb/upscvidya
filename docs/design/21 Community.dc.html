Interaction notes

  • Challenges are auto-verified from real activity (PYQs attempted, missions completed) — no manual check-ins, no fake streaks.
  • Challenge progress bars tick live; finishing stamps a commendation sticker (Profile) and posts a quiet line to the battalion feed.
  • Doubts are topic-tagged; "Open topic →" deep-links into the Topic Reader section under discussion. Accepted answers pin as ANSWERED.
  • Feed is battalion-scoped by default (50 people you already compete with) with an "All aspirants" toggle.
  • Polls: one per user per day; voting reveals results with the fill animation.
  • No DMs, no images in v1 — text doubts + polls only, keeping moderation load near zero (reports go to the Admin queue's FLAGGED tab).
  • Desktop: challenges left rail, doubts feed center at 560px.
