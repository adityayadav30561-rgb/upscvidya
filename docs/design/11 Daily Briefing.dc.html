Interaction notes

  • Calendar strip: green dot = day completed, red = missed, hollow = partial. Swipes back through weeks; tap loads that day's feed with a 200ms crossfade.
  • Cards expand in place (350ms); reading to the end auto-marks read (check fades in) — the button is a shortcut, not a chore.
  • Mini-quiz opens as a sheet; 3 questions, instant feedback, +5 XP each. Completing all items + quizzes = "Briefing complete" on the Home mission card.
  • Missed CA quiz questions enter the revision stack tagged CURRENT AFFAIRS with a shorter interval curve (news decays faster).
  • Category chips filter the feed; sources are informational, not links (offline-first).
  • Past days beyond 30 collapse into a monthly archive list (premium keeps 365 days, free keeps 30 — surfaced in Paywall).
  • Desktop: feed 560px centered; calendar strip becomes a month grid in the right rail.
