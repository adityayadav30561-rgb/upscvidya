Interaction notes

  • Card shows the question first; tap reveals the answer half (dashed fold unfolds, 200ms). Grade buttons appear only after reveal.
  • Swipe left = Again (card tilts red, snaps to back of stack), right = Good (tilts green, flies off). Mastered is deliberately button-only — no accidental retirement.
  • Stack visual depletes as cards clear; final card flies off into a "Stack clear — +40 XP" state with the count-up.
  • "missed 2×" escalates: 3× misses adds a "re-read topic" link on the card.
  • Decay retakes are short quizzes (6–8Q) from the territory's pool; passing restores full colour on the map.
  • Week strip is honest math — skipping visibly doubles tomorrow's number in a 200ms tick.
  • Session caps at 25 cards; beyond that it splits ("14 now · 11 this evening") to protect quality.
  • Desktop: card centered at 420px, grade keys 1/2/3, space reveals.
