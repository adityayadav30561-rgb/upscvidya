Interaction notes

  • Battalions are 50-user cohorts matched on target year + pace, assigned at onboarding — competition stays winnable.
  • Your row stays sticky at the viewport bottom while scrolling ranks 9–50; tapping it scrolls to your true position.
  • Rank changes animate on open: rows FLIP to new positions (350ms), your ▲/▼ delta counts up.
  • Podium builds on entry — pillars rise staggered (3rd, 2nd, 1st) with the crown dropping onto #1.
  • Countdown chip turns from red to pulsing in the final 6 hours; week rollover shows a "Week 29 results" interstitial with your final position + badge if earned.
  • Tapping any aspirant opens a mini-profile: rank, streak, territories held — no chat (heads-down tool, community lives in Screen 21).
  • XP values are weekly, not lifetime — everyone restarts Monday, so a bad week never buries anyone.
  • Desktop: podium left, full 50-row table right with sortable streak/accuracy columns.
