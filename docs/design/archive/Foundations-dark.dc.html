06 · Motion & Interaction Principles

  • Conquest — node fills khaki with a 700ms pop (--ease-pop), single gold particle burst; region % ticks up. Reserved for real achievement only.
  • Answer feedback — border + tint transition 200ms; correct gets a 120ms check draw-in, wrong a 1-cycle 4px shake.
  • Streak increment — flame scales 1→1.15→1 with count roll-up; freeze token consumption shows blue frost sweep.
  • Sheets — topic sheet slides up 350ms; scrim fades to rgba(0,0,0,.5). Rank-up is the only full-screen takeover.
  • Decay — no animation; territories dim quietly. Urgency lives in the revision stack count, not the map.
