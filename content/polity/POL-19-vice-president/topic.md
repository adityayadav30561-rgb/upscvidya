# Vice-President

**Article 63** provides for a Vice-President of India — the second highest constitutional office, modelled on the American Vice-President.

## Functions

- **Ex-officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha** (Article 64); presides over its sittings but votes only to resolve a tie.
- **Acts as President** (Article 65) when a vacancy occurs (death, resignation, removal) — for a maximum of **six months**, within which a new President must be elected. Also discharges the President's functions during temporary absence or illness.
- While acting as President, he does not preside over the Rajya Sabha; the Deputy Chairman does.

## Election (Article 66)

- Electoral college: **elected AND nominated members of BOTH Houses of Parliament**. State legislatures play **no part** — the key contrast with the President's election (which includes elected MLAs and excludes nominated members).
- System: **proportional representation by means of the single transferable vote**, voting by **secret ballot**.
- Election disputes go to the **Supreme Court**, whose decision is final.

## Qualifications, term, removal

- Citizen of India, at least **35 years** of age, qualified for election as a member of the **Rajya Sabha** (contrast: President — Lok Sabha).
- Term: **5 years**; eligible for re-election; resignation addressed to the **President**.
- Removal: resolution of the **Rajya Sabha passed by an effective majority, agreed to by the Lok Sabha** with 14 days' prior notice. The Constitution specifies **no grounds** for removal — and no formal impeachment.
- Oath administered by the **President** or a person appointed by him.

**First Vice-President:** Dr. S. Radhakrishnan (1952-62).
