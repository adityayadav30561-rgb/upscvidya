The Preamble is the introductory statement of the Constitution, based on the Objectives Resolution moved by Jawaharlal Nehru in the Constituent Assembly on 13 December 1946 and adopted on 22 January 1947.

TEXT AND KEYWORDS

It declares India a Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic Republic and secures to all citizens Justice, Liberty, Equality and Fraternity.

FIELD NOTE
Socialist, secular and integrity were added by the 42nd Amendment, 1976 — the only amendment to the Preamble so far.
